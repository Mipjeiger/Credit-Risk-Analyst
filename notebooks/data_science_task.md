- Model Monitoring: Developed and maintained dashboards (Dash, Streamlit, Superset) for model monitoring, ensuring model metrics (Gini, KS, PSI) remain within acceptable thresholds; built and scheduled ETL workflows  for monitoring of model performance; investigated and analysed reasons for declines in performance or other anomalies with database deep dives, and delivered recommendations to relevant stakeholders.
- Model Development: Developed an income estimation model (XGBoost) which improves the existing model’s MAPE by ~78%, minimizing the risk of accepting Debtors with low capacity to pay and ensuring regulatory compliance; developed a propensity model (XGBoost) with an Average Precision  of ~92%, minimizing the cost spent on fetching external vendor scores for Debtors unlikely to make an order; developed challenger Credit Scoring  models (XGBoost, LGBM, Logistic Regression, Random Forest) as benchmarks and baselines for existing “champion” models
- Backtesting and Analysis: Backtested and performed cost-benefit analyses on third-party data and scores, measuring their  impact on bottomline business against cost; accordingly made recommendations to relevant stakeholders to retain/dismiss vendors. 
- Model Evaluation and Reporting: Evaluated model performance and impact on business portfolio (NPF and FPD30 rates); presented and communicated quarterly reports and recommendations  directly to the Board of Directors, and monthly reports to the senior credit manager. 
- Stakeholder Liaison: Liaised with internal stakeholders (credit managers, Board of Directors, other divisions) and external (banks, vendors and regulators) on matters concerning modelling and statistics.
- Automation and Tooling: Developed scripts to streamline and automate various workflows, such as monthly report generation, chart construction, and queries; developed a bot to monitor the database and alert when metrics exceed acceptable thresholds.
